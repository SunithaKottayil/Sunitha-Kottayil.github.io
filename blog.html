My Story

My Journey from Data to Code

Every developer has a story. Here's mine — from discovering the power of data to building solutions that make a real impact.

"I love learning new things and helping others understand complex topics. There's something magical about turning data into stories that people can actually use!"

Hi there! I'm Sunitha 👋 I'm a student developer living in beautiful Aarhus, Denmark. My tech journey started pretty much like everyone else's - I was curious about how things work! I remember the first time I used Excel and thought "wow, this is like magic!" That curiosity led me to Python, then to data visualization, and now I'm learning web development. It's been quite a ride!

What I Love

Figuring out puzzles (both code and real-life ones!), helping friends understand tech stuff, and creating things that actually work. I get really excited when my code runs without errors on the first try - that's the best feeling ever!

My Mission

To make data and coding less scary for everyone! I want to help people see that tech isn't just for "tech people" - it's for anyone who's curious and wants to solve problems in creative ways.

My Approach

I start by asking lots of questions (sometimes too many, my friends say!). Then I break big problems into smaller, manageable pieces. I'm not afraid to Google things or ask for help - that's how we all learn, right?

My Professional Journey

2025 - Present Data Technician & Python Instructor at ReDI School
Teaching Python to other newcomers while still learning myself! It's amazing how much you learn when you teach others. Plus, I get to meet so many interesting people from different backgrounds.
2024 Capstone Project with Arla Foods
Got to work on a real company project! It was nerve-wracking at first, but I learned that real-world problems are actually more interesting than textbook examples. Plus, I got to present my findings to actual business people!
2023 Python Programming Journey Begins
Started learning Python and wow, what a game-changer! I went from "Hello World" to building small tools that actually helped me with my daily tasks. The feeling when your first script works is indescribable!
2020 Data Discovery with Excel & Power BI
My first "aha!" moment with data. I remember creating my first chart in Excel and thinking "this is so cool!" Power BI blew my mind with its interactive dashboards. That's when I knew I wanted to work with data.

When I'm Not Coding...

When I'm not coding, you'll find me reading tech blogs (I'm addicted to Medium articles!), experimenting with new recipes (my latest attempt was homemade pizza - it was... interesting!), or taking walks around Aarhus. I also love helping other students with their coding questions - there's nothing better than that moment when someone finally understands a concept they've been struggling with!
