👋 Hello! I'm Sunitha, a data storyteller who believes every dataset has a narrative waiting to be discovered. My journey began in education, but curiosity led me down the fascinating path of data analytics and software development.

What drives me? The magical moment when complex data transforms into clear, actionable insights that drive real-world decisions. Currently expanding my technical arsenal as a Data Technician student, diving deep into Python, C#, and TypeScript.

Whether it's building predictive models, designing intuitive dashboards, or teaching the next generation of developers, I'm committed to bridging the gap between complex technology and human understanding.
